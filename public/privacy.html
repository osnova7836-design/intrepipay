Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 6, 2025  ·  IntrepiX Group LLC

Overview

TrackPoint is a private, internal business tool operated by IntrepiX Group LLC. It is not a public application and is not offered to the general public. This policy describes how data is handled within the application.

Data We Access

TrackPoint connects to QuickBooks Online and Jobber on behalf of the authorized business operator. In doing so, it may access:

— Invoice and payment records from Jobber
— Payment and bank transaction records from QuickBooks Online
— Bank statement exports uploaded directly by the operator

How We Use Data

Data accessed from QuickBooks Online and Jobber is used solely to perform payment reconciliation for the operator's business. It is not analyzed for any secondary purpose, sold, shared with third parties, or retained beyond the active session.

Data Storage

OAuth access tokens for QuickBooks Online and Jobber are stored locally in encrypted environment files on the server. No customer financial data is permanently stored in TrackPoint's database.

Data Sharing

TrackPoint does not share, sell, rent, or disclose any data accessed through Intuit APIs to any third party. All data remains within the operator's own systems.

Security

API credentials are stored as environment variables and are never exposed in client-side code or logs. All connections to Intuit and Jobber APIs are made over HTTPS.
